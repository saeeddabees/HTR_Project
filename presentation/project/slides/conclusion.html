All · Conclusion

Pretraining wins on accuracy; attention CTC wins on size

What we showed

Each idea helps: BiLSTM −4.2 pp, attention −2.5 pp, augmentation −2.9 pp CER. TrOCR reaches 7.0% CER.

Trade-off

Attention + Aug: 1.5 M parameters, 6 MB, fast on CPU. TrOCR: 385 M parameters, 1.4 GB, needs a GPU.

Limitations

One TrOCR run; budgets differ across the main CTC runs; no writer-overlap check; greedy decoding only.
